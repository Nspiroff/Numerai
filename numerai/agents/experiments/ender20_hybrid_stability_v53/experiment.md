# Ender-20 Hybrid Stability Experiment (v5.3)

**Started:** 2026-08-03
**State:** completed; no calibration-eligible hybrid

## Research question

Can a fixed blend of the official Ender-20 benchmark and the validated TabM
residual preserve useful BMC while bringing full and recent BMC drawdown below
the production gate?

## Motivation

The prior six-round neural search established TabM `k=64`, three 512-wide ReLU
blocks, dropout `0.1`, and a 500,000-row cap as the strongest tested residual
architecture. Its consecutive-era OOF result has BMC `0.005798`, recent BMC
`0.003723`, Corr `0.009358`, BMC Sharpe `0.5087`, and benchmark correlation
`0.0725`, but BMC maximum drawdown `0.300982` failed the frozen `<0.15` gate.

The new idea is deployment-feasible because Numerai supplies
`live_benchmark_models` to the hosted prediction function. A hybrid therefore
does not need to embed or rerun a large LightGBM model.

## Baselines

- Quality reference: residual-only `scale_disk_tabm_k64_train500k`.
- Direct-prediction reference: official `v53_lgbm_ender20`.
- Tutorial account models (`serenity_edge`, `ender_harmony`) remain untouched
  and are not directly comparable to this exact OOF protocol.

## Protocol

The complete frozen transform, five residual-weight variants, calibration
selection rule, holdout gate, and deployment boundary are recorded in
[`gate.md`](gate.md). Results will be generated by a deterministic analysis
script and an executed reader-facing notebook.

## Results

The deterministic evaluator joined all `5,112,039` OOF rows to the exact
feature-store manifest with one-to-one IDs, exact era/target agreement, finite
signals, and the frozen residual prediction semantics. The cohort contains 855
eras from `0371` through `1225`; calibration is eras `0371`-`1025` and the
locked 200-era holdout starts at `1026`.

`hybrid_w35` was the closest candidate. It cleared the calibration drawdown,
BMC-retention, Corr-retention, and benchmark-similarity checks, but its BMC
Sharpe was `0.4422`, below the frozen strict `> 0.45` threshold. Every higher
residual weight failed the `< 0.15` calibration drawdown ceiling and the 90%
benchmark-Corr retention check. The evaluator therefore selected no candidate.

For transparency only, not selection, `hybrid_w35` had holdout BMC `0.001838`,
BMC Sharpe `0.3341`, maximum drawdown `0.0587`, and Corr `0.022286`. It would
also miss the strict holdout Sharpe threshold of `> 0.35`.

The executed analysis notebook is
[`ender20_hybrid_stability_analysis.ipynb`](ender20_hybrid_stability_analysis.ipynb).
Machine-readable local outputs are under `results/` and are intentionally
ignored by Git because they are generated data artifacts.

### Independent validation

An independent read-only audit reconciled all 5,985 candidate-era metric rows
to the JSON/CSV summaries within `4.44e-16`. It also caught that the repository's
generic prediction-correlation helper applies asymmetric target-style Numerai
Corr: the benchmark initially scored only `0.9468` against itself. The evaluator
was corrected to symmetric per-era Spearman correlation, a focused self-correlation
test was added, and the complete 5.1-million-row evaluation was rerun. The
benchmark now scores exactly `1.0` against itself and the null selection is
unchanged; every candidate still fails an independent Sharpe, drawdown, or Corr
retention check.

## Decision

`NOT_PROMOTION_ELIGIBLE`. The predeclared experiment stops without a winner;
no hybrid pickle was packaged and nothing was uploaded to Numerai. The existing
TabM pickle remains an experimental, locally validated artifact rather than a
production-approved model.
